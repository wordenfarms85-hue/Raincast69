THE PROBLEM

As a fourth-generation farmer in eastern Colorado, I spent years relying on generic weather apps that had no idea what a planting window was, couldn't tell me if it was safe to spray, and treated a dryland wheat field the same as a golf course in Florida.

They gave me a seven-day forecast. What I needed was GDU accumulation, spray inversion risk, 14-day blended precip, and how this year compares to 1988. No app did that. So I built one.

THE MISSION

Agricast gives professional farmers and agronomists the weather intelligence they actually need — multi-model precipitation blends, real GDU tracking, soil moisture at four depths, spray windows with inversion detection, and 75 years of analog history — all in one platform built specifically for the field.

No generic forecasts. No consumer fluff. Just the data that drives better decisions on real ground.

THE TEAM

Agricast is built and maintained by one person — but shaped by generations of experience. My family has farmed eastern Colorado for four generations. Their knowledge, their questions, and their hard-won instincts about the land are baked into every feature this platform has.
